Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Orbit ("we", "our" or "us") is an Android app that helps you plan office days and personal commitments across the month. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Data you give us

When you set up Orbit you provide:

  • Your office-day target, preferred weekdays and working hours
  • Recurring activities (gym, study, sports, etc.) and their schedule preferences
  • Holidays and fixed events you add manually
  • Check-in records and optional notes you write for each activity

If you create an account, we also store your email address to identify your plan and let you restore it on a new device.

2. Data collected automatically

We collect minimal technical data to keep the app working:

  • Crash reports — stack traces and device model/OS version when the app crashes, so we can fix bugs. No personal data is included.
  • Anonymous usage events — which features you use (e.g. "re-plan triggered"), without any identifying information. This helps us decide what to improve.

We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to third parties.

3. Calendar access

Orbit can write your planned days to your device's calendar. This permission is optional — you grant it in Android settings and can revoke it at any time. We read your calendar only to avoid creating duplicate events; we never upload calendar data to our servers.

4. AI re-planning

When you describe a change in natural language (e.g. "friend's wedding Saturday"), your message and the affected portion of your current plan are sent to our AI service to generate a suggested re-plan. We do not store these messages beyond the time needed to return a response, and they are never used to train models.

5. How we store and protect your data

Your plan data is stored on your device and, if you are signed in, in our cloud database. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the cloud provider. We apply least-privilege access controls so that only the systems that need your data can reach it.

6. Data retention

Your plan data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Settings screen. Anonymised crash and usage data is retained for up to 12 months.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Orbit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here with an updated date. If the changes are significant, we will notify you in the app.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or to exercise your data rights:
